# Chapter 04: Measuring Skill-Set Complexity

## Practical exercises

### E01

Conceptual: Explain why context length alone is insufficient to measure the complexity of agentic AI tasks. Illustrate with an example involving high evidence dispersion.

### E02

Practical: Extend the lab to include trust-aware tool selection, where each tool has a dynamic trust score based on recent performance and availability.

### E03

Analytical: Given a set of tasks with high evidence dispersion but low reasoning depth, predict how an agent’s performance might differ from tasks with low dispersion but high reasoning depth.

### E04

Critical Thinking: Discuss the implications of the "lost-in-the-middle" effect for real-world applications such as clinical decision support or legal document analysis.

### E05

Research: Survey a recent agentic AI system (e.g., LangChain, AutoGen) and describe how it manages skill-set complexity and tool orchestration. ---

## Review questions
