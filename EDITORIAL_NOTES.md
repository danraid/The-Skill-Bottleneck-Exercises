# Editorial Provenance and Scope

The exercises and review questions were transferred from Daniel Raaid's canonical 20-chapter textbook manuscript. Their worked-answer notes give chapter-supported model reasoning and implementation acceptance criteria. Several questions are open-ended and have no unique answer; alternative designs are valid when justified and tested.

The 14 laboratory reference solutions and tests were transferred from the author's original engineering-lab pipeline, together with recorded deterministic test outcomes. The teaching fixtures do not claim that skill catalogue cardinality determines real-world LLM accuracy, nor do they replace independently controlled scientific experiments.

**Privacy boundary:** the book's complete private manuscript is not stored in this public companion repository; only exercise content, model-answer notes and laboratory materials are published.
